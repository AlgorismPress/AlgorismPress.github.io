Color system

Primary green #1F7A5C
Secondary blue #5170FF
Reading ink #173F3F

Typography

Cinzel is for the wordmark, display headings, and logo lettering. EB Garamond is for editorial reading. JetBrains Mono is for technical labels and metadata.

Proper use

  • Keep clear space around the mark.
  • Use green on white or white on green.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, or redraw the mark.

Use the full wordmark when the name needs to be clear, and choose a monogram when space is limited.
